# Independent Review Disagreement

## Trigger

The producing assignment and independent reviewer disagree on a material requirement, risk, finding, evidence class, or acceptance result.

## Procedure

1. Preserve both positions and their exact evidence without forcing consensus.
2. Confirm reviewer independence, read scope, role, route, and assignment identity.
3. Reproduce disputed facts with deterministic checks where possible.
4. Classify the disagreement as factual, interpretive, risk-acceptance, or authority-related.
5. Escalate protected decisions and unresolved material findings to the Sole Human Owner.

## Resume Criteria

Resume only after the disposition, accepted evidence, remaining dissent, and accountable decision are recorded.

## Required Record

Producer result, review result, disputed claims, reproduction evidence, disposition, remaining risk, and decision reference.
