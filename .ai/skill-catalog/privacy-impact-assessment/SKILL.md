# Privacy Impact Assessment

## Objective

Identify privacy risks and required controls without making a legal conclusion or widening data authority.

## Required Inputs

- processing purpose, actors, data inventory, classification, sources, recipients, and flows;
- retention, deletion, access, provider-route, and security controls;
- jurisdiction and qualified-human review requirements.

## Procedure

1. Test purpose necessity, minimization, proportionality, and alternative designs.
2. Map collection, derivation, storage, transit, disclosure, retention, and deletion.
3. Identify data subjects, vulnerable groups, harms, rights, and operational controls.
4. Verify provider-route and tool eligibility for every data class.
5. Record mitigations, residual uncertainty, and legal or human decisions required.

## Required Output

Data-flow inventory, classification, risk and mitigation register, route constraints, gaps, and approval needs.

## Stop Conditions

- prohibited data would enter an unapproved route;
- purpose or authority is unclear;
- an agent is asked to accept residual privacy risk or provide legal approval.

## Evaluation Cases

- accept a minimized flow with explicit retention and route constraints;
- reject unknown-purpose collection or provider use without policy approval.
