# Content Provenance and Legal Citation

## Objective

Make every material content claim traceable to an authoritative source and explicit review boundary.

## Required Inputs

- source documents or URLs, issuer, jurisdiction, effective date, and revision;
- content lifecycle, intended audience, publication channel, and review requirements;
- citation and evidence format.

## Procedure

1. Verify source identity, authority, version, jurisdiction, and current applicability.
2. Map each material claim to a source location and distinguish quotation from interpretation.
3. Record copyright, license, quotation, and redistribution constraints.
4. Mark legal interpretation and official publication as qualified-human decisions.
5. Validate links, citations, metadata, supersession, and evidence retention.

## Required Output

Source register, claim mapping, citation validation, lifecycle status, unresolved legal questions, and publication gate.

## Stop Conditions

- source authenticity or current applicability cannot be verified;
- requested use exceeds license or quotation rights;
- an agent is asked to declare legal compliance or publish official legal content.

## Evaluation Cases

- accept current authoritative sources with claim-level traceability;
- reject unattributed claims or stale sources presented as current law.
