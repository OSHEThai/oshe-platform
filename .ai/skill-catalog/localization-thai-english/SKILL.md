# Thai-English Localization

## Objective

Produce traceable Thai and English content without changing technical, safety, legal, or lifecycle meaning.

## Required Inputs

- authoritative source and target locale;
- terminology registry, non-translatable identifiers, and domain references;
- required output format and layout authority.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the authoritative source version and language direction.
2. Build or reuse controlled terminology and preserve identifiers, units, and citations.
3. Mark unknown or ambiguous facts rather than inventing content.
4. Produce separate EN and TH files when governed; do not combine languages unless explicitly required.
5. Read back content and render every layout-sensitive page before visual QA claims.

## Required Output

Localized artifact, terminology decisions, source mapping, unresolved ambiguity, content checks, and rendered QA evidence when applicable.

## Stop Conditions

- source authority or revision is unclear;
- translation would create a legal, safety, or compliance claim;
- required qualified human language or domain review is unavailable.

## Evaluation Cases

- accept separate traceable TH and EN outputs with layout QA;
- reject invented terminology or silent omission of source content.
